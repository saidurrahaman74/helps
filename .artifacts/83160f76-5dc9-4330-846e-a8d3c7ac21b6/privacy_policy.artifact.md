By downloading, installing, or using Smart Help, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use the app.

## 2. Information We Collect
To provide comprehensive health tracking, emergency tools, and clinical calculators, Smart Help may collect or process the following types of information:

### A. Personal & Medical Information (Provided by You)
* **Profile Information:** Name, age, gender, blood group.
* **Health Vitals & Trackers:** Blood pressure, heart rate (pulse), blood oxygen (SpO2), body temperature, blood glucose, insulin logs, fluid intake, weight, height, BMI, and step count.
* **Clinical & Emergency Data:** Medical ID details (allergies, chronic conditions, current medications), emergency contact phone numbers, doctor appointments, vaccination records, and period/pregnancy tracking data.
* **Prescriptions & Notes:** Scanned prescriptions, medical documents, voice notes, and lab test reports stored in your Medical Vault.

### B. Device & Permissions Data
To deliver core features, Smart Help requests specific device permissions:
* **Camera:** Used for scanning prescription barcodes/images, scanning food items for diet analysis, and scanning emergency QR codes.
* **Storage / Media:** Used for saving and exporting medical reports as PDFs or attaching vault documents.
* **Location:** Used exclusively for locating nearby emergency services, hospitals, or emergency directories (with user consent).
* **Microphone / Audio:** Used for voice search and recording voice notes.
* **Notifications & Exact Alarms:** Used for medicine reminders, pill trackers, and period/appointment alerts.
* **Activity Recognition:** Used for step counting and physical activity tracking.

---

## 3. How We Store and Secure Your Data
* **Local Storage First:** The majority of your personal health data, vitals, and medical records are stored securely on your device using encrypted local databases (**Room Database**). This ensures that your private health information remains under your direct control.
* **Security Measures:** Sensitive screens (such as Medical ID) implement security flags (`FLAG_SECURE`) to prevent unauthorized screenshots or screen recording.
* **Caregiver Sync (Optional):** If you use the Caregiver Pairing or Sync feature, data transmitted to linked caregivers is done over secure, encrypted network channels with your explicit authorization.
* **No Selling of Data:** SR Creation Solution **never** sells, rents, or trades your personal or health data to third parties, advertisers, or data brokers.

---

## 4. How We Use Your Information
We use the collected information solely to:
* Provide and maintain personalized health tracking and medical reminders.
* Generate health summaries, BMI calculations, insulin calculations, and PDF reports for your personal use or consultation with your physician.
* Provide life-saving emergency triage guidelines, SOS dialers, and emergency directories.
* Sync emergency data with your designated caregivers (when enabled by you).
